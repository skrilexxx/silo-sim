# Experiments (M15, lean plan)

Written down before any of them runs (T15.4). Every experiment uses **seeds 1–30**, the same in every scenario, so differences between scenarios come from the factor, not from noise (common random numbers; ODD §1.2, D11 revised 2026-11-04). Scenarios run 50 warm-up and **150 measured years**; the canon validation runs canon's 300. All of it is run by `analysis/m15.sh`, which saves both reports of each batch in `runs/m15/<name>.txt`:

- `silosim report` — the §8 baseline targets and the canon patterns C1–C4 (C5 with Silo 1 on);
- `silosim report --outcomes` — the outcome metrics of §1.2 with 95% intervals, and the Kaplan–Meier curves.

Claims are comparative and conditional on the model's rules (ODD §1.2). A difference counts as a result when the 95% intervals of the two scenarios don't overlap; when they do, the report says so: that is a result too.

## T15.3 Canon validation

- **Question:** do the canon patterns hold at the show's settings?
- **Scenario:** `scenarios/canon.yaml` (300 measured years, The Order, a scripted cleaning failure in year 150); C5 from E4.
- **Metric:** C1–C4 pass or fail as printed by `silosim report`; C5 from E4's batch. C3's strike half and C4 are reported as they are (Adam, 2026-11-04).

## E1 Peace without shocks (Q1)

- **Question:** how long does peace last when nothing scripted happens?
- **Scenario:** `experiments/e1-noshock.yaml` (base without its cleaning failure).
- **Metric:** survival to the first rebellion (Kaplan–Meier median, S at 25, 50, … years); rebellions per century.

## E2 The trigger effect of a visible failure (Q1, C2)

- **Question:** does a cleaner who walks away set off a rebellion?
- **Scenarios:** `experiments/base.yaml` (a cleaning failure on day 45,625, measured year 75) against `e1-noshock.yaml`.
- **Metric:** the share of runs with a rebellion starting in the 5 years from day 45,625, in each arm (from `rebellions.jsonl`), with Wilson intervals; C2 in base's report.

## E3 Regime response (Q3)

- **Question:** which response keeps the silo alive and quiet: none, The Order, crackdown, reform, memory wipe, or mixed?
- **Scenarios:** `e3-none.yaml`, `base.yaml` (The Order), `e3-crackdown.yaml`, `e3-reform.yaml`, `e3-wipe.yaml`, `e3-mixed.yaml` (values by the factor of 2, ODD §6.15).
- **Metric:** survival to collapse; share of measured time in a rebellion; rebellions per century; share of endings. This is M13's milestone check: do the curves differ by more than seed noise?

## E4 Silo 1 (Q2, Q4, C5)

- **Question:** does Silo 1's threat make Silo 18 more stable or more fragile?
- **Scenarios:** `base.yaml` (Silo 1 off) against `e4-silo1.yaml` (full, ODD §6.16).
- **Metric:** survival to collapse and its causes (`safeguard` among them); rebellions per century; share of time in a rebellion; C5 (5–50% of rebellions end in collapse) in e4's report.

## E5 Governance (Q3)

- **Question:** does choosing the Mayor by election, or for a 5-year term rather than for life, keep the silo quieter?
- **Scenarios:** `base.yaml` (appointment, 5 years), `e5-election.yaml`, `e5-life.yaml`, `e5-election-life.yaml`.
- **Metric:** rebellions per century and share of time in a rebellion. Elections lift L by λ\_elect (ODD §6.15); a life term removes regular change, which in this model has no other effect, so a difference between the term settings would point to a defect.

## T15.2 Sensitivity, one at a time

- **Question:** which of the key part-2 guesses move the outcomes?
- **Scenarios:** `base.yaml` with one parameter at −50% and +50%, 12 cells, generated by the script. The parameters: the doubt taught, 1 − L\_edu (0.2: L\_edu 0.9 and 0.7, since L\_edu itself can't go above 1), δ (0.15), π (0.002), T (0.1), s\_DD (0.2, the peacetime value; The Order's 0.6 is unchanged) and p\_fail (0.05).
- **Metric:** rebellions per century, share of time in a rebellion and collapses, against base; a parameter matters when either cell's interval leaves base's.
